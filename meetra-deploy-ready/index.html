How it works

From sign-up to a smoother commute

Meetra makes it simple to join your route community, reserve and pay for a trip, then board at your designated stop.

01

Sign up

Create your passenger account and complete Meetra’s passenger verification so we can prepare your access to the service.

Your details help us support a trusted passenger community.
02

Join your route channel

Get added to the community channel for your route, where you can receive trip updates and important travel information.

Stay up to date with your route.
03

Pre-book your trip

Choose your route and reserve a seat before you travel. Daily trips are planned for morning and evening at specific departure times.

Book ahead to plan your commute.
04

Make your payment

Follow the payment instructions provided for your trip. Your booking is confirmed once your payment has been verified.

Payment details will be shown during booking.
05

Board at your bus stop

Arrive at your designated stop for the scheduled departure. Your Meetra-marked bus will be waiting, so there’s no need to hustle for a ride.

Board your pre-booked trip with less peak-hour stress.

FAQs

Good to know before you go

What Meetra is, how it works, and what to expect as we prepare to launch.

Meetra is a mobility service being built to make everyday city commutes more seamless. We bring together comfort, affordability and community for people travelling in the same direction.
Meetra builds on the public transport systems and shared routes people already use, adding a more coordinated experience with pre-booked trips, clear departure times and designated boarding stops.
Meetra is for commuters who want a practical way to travel around the city. We’re especially focused on young professionals and people travelling along shared routes, with room for a community of people pursuing similar goals.
The passenger waitlist lets you tell us where and how you travel. It helps us understand commuter interest and plan the service. Joining the waitlist is not a trip booking or a confirmed seat.
Trip booking is coming soon. When the service is ready, the Book a trip button will take you to the booking form and we’ll share the details through Meetra’s official channels.
We’re using passenger interest to understand which routes to prioritize. Share your usual starting area and destination in the waitlist form; we’ll announce confirmed service areas as plans develop.
Affordability is central to Meetra, but fares and payment details will be shared when routes and trip bookings are announced. Joining the waitlist does not require payment.
Meetra works with public transport buses that are in good condition. We verify vehicles and drivers before they are onboarded to the service.
Drivers and vehicles are verified before onboarding, and only drivers with a valid driver’s licence are onboarded. Passengers are also verified before using the service. We’ll share the verification steps and requirements with passengers before bookings open.
Meetra buses will carry our stickers so you can identify them. For a pre-booked trip, the bus will be parked at the designated bus stop. Your seat is reserved, so you won’t have to hustle to get on board during the peak-hour rush.
The planned service has two trips each day: one in the morning and one in the evening. Trips are pre-booked, with specific departure times for each route. We’ll share the route schedules when booking opens.
Meetra Technologies Ltd. is a software technology company developing innovative mobility, logistics and payment solutions. Its flagship product, the Meetra App, is a mobility platform designed to connect passengers travelling along the same routes and help them plan and coordinate their journeys through seamless booking, boarding and payment. The app supports Meetra’s shared bus service; trip booking will become available as routes launch.
